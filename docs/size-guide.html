Size Guide

Use the measurements below to choose the perfect size. All measurements are in centimeters.

How to Measure

Lay the garment flat on a smooth surface. Measure Width across the chest from seam to seam, and Length from the top of the shoulder to the hem. Compare to the tables below to find your best fit.
